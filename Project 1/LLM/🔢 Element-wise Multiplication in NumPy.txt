How can I perform element-wise multiplication on numpy arrays? Suppose I have an array g, and I want to square each element, how do I do that?


[GPT]

Use NumPy’s vectorized operations. For element-wise multiplication of arrays, the operator * works element-wise. To square each element of g, you can use any of these:

- g * g
- g ** 2
- np.square(g)
- np.power(g, 2)

Examples:
- Squaring into a new array:
  g = np.array([1, 2, 3])
  squared = g ** 2          # [1, 4, 9]
  # or squared = np.square(g)

- In-place squaring (no extra allocation):
  g **= 2
  # or np.square(g, out=g)

Element-wise multiplication of two arrays:
  a = np.array([1, 2, 3])
  b = np.array([4, 5, 6])
  a * b                      # [4, 10, 18]

Broadcasting also works:
  A = np.arange(9).reshape(3, 3)
  v = np.array([1, 10, 100])
  A * v                      # each row multiplied by v element-wise

Notes:
- For matrix multiplication, use @ or np.matmul, not *.
- Be mindful of dtype. Squaring large integers can overflow; use a larger or floating dtype if needed:
  g = g.astype(np.int64)  # or np.float64
- You can avoid temporary arrays by using the out parameter:
  np.multiply(a, b, out=dest) or np.square(g, out=g).
