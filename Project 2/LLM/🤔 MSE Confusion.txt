When referring to the mean squared error as MSE, do you get a MSE or an MSE?


[GPT]

Use “an MSE.”

- We use “an” before words that start with a vowel sound.
- The initialism “MSE” is pronounced “em-ess-ee,” which starts with a vowel sound (“em”), so “an MSE” is correct.
- If you say the full term, you’d use “a mean squared error.”

Examples:
- “We report an MSE of 0.12.”
- “This model has a lower MSE.”
- “We achieved a mean squared error of 0.12.”
