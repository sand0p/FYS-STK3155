What is the expression for the R^2 score, if it is a function of true values y and predicted values \hat{y}?


[GPT]

**Coefficient of determination ($R^2$)**

```math
R^2 \;=\; 1 \;-\; \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \left(y_i - \hat{y}_i\right)^2}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} \left(y_i - \bar{y}\right)^2}
```

- $\bar{y}$ is the mean of the true values: $\bar{y} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} y_i$.
- The numerator is the residual sum of squares (SS_res).
- The denominator is the total sum of squares (SS_tot).

Vector form:

```math
R^2 \;=\; 1 \;-\; \frac{\lVert \mathbf{y} - \hat{\mathbf{y}} \rVert_2^2}{\lVert \mathbf{y} - \bar{y}\mathbf{1} \rVert_2^2}
```
